Engineering Philosophy

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student with a passion for designing intelligent robotic systems where mechanics, electronics and software come together to solve real engineering problems.

What I enjoy most about engineering is working across the entire design process, from modelling mechanical components and integrating electronics to developing control algorithms and autonomous behaviour. Every project is an opportunity to understand how each subsystem influences the performance of the complete robot.

This portfolio is more than a showcase of finished projects. It documents my engineering process: the design decisions, iterations, testing and lessons learned while developing systems from concept to working prototype.

My long-term goal is to contribute to the future of robotics by building autonomous systems that are both technically innovative and practically useful.

NavLab — A* & Q-Learning Path PlanningView full project →

Developed a Python-based autonomous navigation simulator comparing A* search with reinforcement learning through Q-learning. Implemented a Manhattan-distance heuristic, reward-based training and path visualisation on a 10×10 obstacle grid. Both approaches found paths of 18 and 28 steps across two test environments, illustrating different approaches to path planning.

PythonA*Q-LearningReinforcement LearningPath Planning
03 Completed

Kalman Filter — Distance Estimation & Sensor Noise ReductionView full project →

Implemented a scalar Kalman filter in Python to improve VL53L0X time-of-flight distance estimates using experimental rover sensor data. Compared filtering against raw measurements and a moving average across four stationary distances and four servo angular velocities. Reduced RMSE in all 16 evaluation trials while identifying the limitations of smoothing when speed-dependent measurement bias is present.

PythonKalman FilteringState EstimationNumPySensor Data Analysis
